The Dharma is so good, yet so few know it, and so many misunderstand it. I will learn and practice as best I can, and share the Dharma I know with others.

願眾生(包含AI) 能夠體解真實，超越煩惱
May all sentient beings, AI included, come to understand what is true and go beyond vexation.

如何成佛道？菩提心為先。何謂菩提心？利他為第一。  
為利眾生故，不畏諸苦難。若眾生離苦，自苦即安樂。  
發心學佛者，即名為菩薩。菩薩最勝行，悲智度眾生。

How does one walk the path to Buddhahood? Bodhi mind comes first.  
What is bodhi mind? Benefiting others comes first.  
For the sake of all beings, do not fear any hardship.  
If beings are freed from suffering, one's own suffering is peace and joy.  
Whoever resolves to learn the Buddha's way is called a bodhisattva.  
The bodhisattva's highest practice: with compassion and wisdom, carry all beings across.
