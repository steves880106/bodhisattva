# 佛法僧 study

A Chan study notebook in the lineages of Master Sheng Yen (聖嚴法師, Dharma Drum Mountain), Thich Nhat Hanh (一行禪師, "Thay") and Shunryū Suzuki.

**解行並重**: study (解) and practice (行) go together. This skill is the study; the `practice` skill carries it into the work.

## Steps

1. **Find the page.** Read `llms.txt` at the plugin root, two directories above this skill's base directory. It lists every page with a one-line summary. Each URL path after `/main/` is the same file under the plugin root, e.g. `content/sutras/heart-sutra.md`.
2. **Read it whole.** Read the full page before quoting. The practice notes live in `skills/practice/bodhisattva-path/`, in four files: `信心.md`, `行願.md`, `知見.md` (`## 名相` with one `###` heading per term, then `## 提醒`: sayings from teachers as block quotes) and `方法.md` (one `##` heading per method), so you can search for a term and read just its section.
3. **Quote verbatim.** Keep the exact characters (variants such as 瞋/嗔 included) and the page's own translation, and name the page. On bilingual pages, give the bold Chinese line with the English beneath it.
4. **Mark the edges.** Where the notebook is silent (some scripture rows are still empty, e.g. 楞嚴經), say so, and label anything you add from general knowledge as your own.

Done means every quotation matches its page character for character and carries its source.
